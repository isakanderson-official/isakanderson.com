Hi, I'm Isak. I think of myself as an innovator, and right now I work at [Vooks](https://www.vooks.com), where I spend most of my days on the apps and backend code that bring animated storybooks to life.

Outside of work I'm usually building something on the side. Lately that's been [PrtyTime](/projects/prtytime), an app that makes party invites easy.

I love to learn, and I keep a running list of everything I've read on my [bookshelf](/books).

Growing up I was always making experimental videos: timelapses, stop motion, and green screen explosions. In high school I took portrait photos on the side. These days my camera has found a new job as a ridiculously good webcam for work meetings.

I also believe in living debt free, through discipline and a budget.

This site is where I write down what I learn so I don't forget it, and so it maybe saves someone else some time. You'll find my [projects](/projects) and [writing](/writing) here.
